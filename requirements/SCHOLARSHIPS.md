# Beaverton Rotary Foundation Scholarships

There are 4 types of scholarship available to applicants.  They are:

## Academic
Academic Scholarships are typically used for graduating high school senior who are attending a college or university located in Oregon. Applicants can receive up to $6,000 per year if selected from the selection committee.  These applications can be renewed on an annual basis.


### All Applications
All applications need to collect personal information:
Personal Information
- First, Middle, and Last Name
- Home Address
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Parent(s) or Legal Guardian(s)

### Data
Data for the Academic scholarship is grouped into categories of information.

Education Information
- High school attended (Aloha, Beaverton, Mountainside, Southridge, Sunset, Westview, or other ), radio button, text
- Cumulative GPA, text
- Weighted GPA, text
- ACT Score (optional), text
- SAT Score (optional), text
- Advisor/Counselor, text
- Adivsor Phone  Number, phone_number
- Intended College or University, text
- Field of Study, text
- Academic Honors (optional), text
- College Credits (optional), text

Finance Information
- Intended College or Institution, text
- Tuition and Fees Estimate, numeric (U.S. Dollars)
- Book and Supplies Estimate, numeric (U.S. Dollars)
- Other Costs (estimate), numeric (U.S. Dollars)
- Total Costs  (sum of previous 4 items)
- Estimated Family Contribution, numeric (U.S. Dollars)
- Student Loans (if any), numeric (U.S. Dollars)
- Other scholarships, numeric (U.S. Dollars)
- Other resources, numeric (U.S. Dollars)
- Total estimated resources (Sum  of previous 4 items))

File Uplaod Information
- Current high school transcript, file upload
- Resume of the applicant, file upload
- A Writing Sample (subject to be determed for each Application Window), file upload or textarea
- Letter(s) or recommendation (1 or more), file upload

Additional Information
- Extracurricular activities, textarea
- Miscellaneous information, textarea
-- Is there anything else the applicant wants us to know

## Academic Renewal
Renewal scholarships are awarded to selected applicants who have received their first Academi scholarship.  Academic scholarships can renew annually for up to 3 years, allowing applicants 4 years of total scholarship awards.

## CEYP
Continuing Education for Young Parents is a special type of scholarship awarded to young women who have had a child prior to graduating high school.  This scholarship is intended to help these young women find a suitable career to provide for their family.

### Data

Personal Information
- In addition all previous personal information collected for all applications, add date-of-birth

Family Information
- Mother's full name (first, middle, last)
- Mother's address
- Mother's occupation
- Father's name (first, middle, last) - optional
- Father's address (if different)
- Father's Occupation
