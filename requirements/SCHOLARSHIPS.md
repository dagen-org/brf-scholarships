# Beaverton Rotary Foundation Scholarships

There are 4 types of scholarship available to applicants.  They are:

## Academic
Academic Scholarships are typically used for graduating high school senior who are attending a college or university located in Oregon. Applicants can receive up to $6,000 per year if selected from the selection committee.  These applications can be renewed on an annual basis.

### Data
Data for the Academic scholarship is grouped into categories of information.

Personal Information
- First, Middle, and Last Name
- Home Address
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Parent(s) or Legal Guardian(s)

Education Information
- High school attended (Aloha, Beaverton, Mountainside, Southridge, Sunset, Westview, or other )
- Cumulative GPA
- Weighted GPA
- ACT Score (optional)
- SAT Score (optional)
- Advisor/Counselor
- Adivor Phone  Number
- Intended College
- Field of Study
- Academic Honors (optional)
- College Credits (optional)

Finance Information
- Intended College or Institution
- Tuition and Fees Estimate
- Book and Supplies Estimate
- Other Costs (estimate)
- Estimated Family Contribution
- Student Loans (if any)
- Other scholarships
- Other resources
- Total estimated resources

File Uplaod Information
- Current high school transcript
- Resume of the applicant
- A Writing Sample (subject to be determed for each Application Window)
- Letter(s) or recommendation (1 or more)

Additional Information
- Extracurricular activities
- Miscellaneous information
-- Is there anything else the applicant wants us to know

## Academic Renewal
Renewal scholarships are awarded to selected applicants who have received their first Academi scholarship.  Academic scholarships can renew annually for up to 3 years, allowing applicants 4 years of total scholarship awards.

### Data
Personal Information 
- Same as personal information for Academic Scholarshipis

Education Information
- High School (see list above)
- Cumulative GPA
- Weighted GPA
- Current College/University
- Current field of study
- Academic honors
- Credits earned

Finance Information
- Current Institution
- Tuition and Fees
- Books and Supplies
- Living expenses
- Other costs
- Total estimated costs
- Family contribution
- Student loans
- Other scholarships
- Other resources
- Total estimated resources


File Uploads
- Current year transcript
- Written Presentation


## CEYP
Continuing Education for Young Parents is a special type of scholarship awarded to young women who have had a child prior to graduating high school.  This scholarship is intended to help these young women find a suitable career to provide for their family.

### Data

Personal Information
- First, Middle, and Last Name
- Date of Birth
- Mailing Address
- Phone Number
- Email ADdress

Family Information
- Mother's full name (first, middle, last)
- Mother's address
- Mother's occupation
- Father's name (first, middle, last) - optional
- Father's address (if different)
- Father's Occupation



## Vocational
Vocational scholarships are itended for students who do not wish to attend a 4 year college or university.  Vocational scholarships may attend a trade school, beauty school, or other such organizations to promote a career in the trades.
